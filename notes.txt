1. Chunking
Large document ko smaller meaningful pieces (chunks) mein divide karte hain, taaki retrieval ke time relevant portion hi LLM ko diya ja sake.

2. Embeddings
Text ko numerical vector mein convert karte hain jo uske semantic meaning ko represent karta hai.

3. Similarity Search
Query embedding ko stored chunk embeddings ke saath compare karke semantically similar chunks find karte hain. Cosine similarity commonly use hoti hai.

4. FAISS
FAISS (Facebook AI Similarity Search) vectors ko index karke efficient similarity search karne ki library hai.
